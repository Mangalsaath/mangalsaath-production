# Testing Guide – v2.5.0

1. Login and open Dashboard > Complete/Edit Profile.
2. Confirm separate First name and Surname boxes.
3. Confirm DOB Day/Month/Year dropdowns show adult past years.
4. Confirm dropdowns for gender, marital status, religion, caste, education, career and location.
5. Enter height in centimetres; values below 100 or above 250 must be rejected.
6. Complete partner-preference dropdowns and save.
7. Confirm the profile card displays the combined first name and surname and saved details.
