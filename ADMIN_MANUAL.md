# Administrator Manual

## Payment approval
1. Sign in using the administrator account.
2. Open **Admin Console**.
3. Review **Pending UPI payments**.
4. Match the displayed UTR and amount against the bank/UPI statement.
5. Approve only after successful verification.
6. Approval activates the selected membership and calculates its expiry automatically.
7. Reject unverifiable payments. The member receives a notification.

## Identity verification
Use the verification queue to approve, reject, or request more information. A reason is mandatory for rejection or requests for information.

## Backups
Copy `data/db.json` to secure storage daily during the initial launch period and before every deployment.
